# M4 spikes: Android's unlock, the home screen, the lock, the phone role

The measurements of `docs/plan-m4.md`, step G0, on 2026-10-27:
- **A, unlocking:** Android's lock and a key bound to it, with and without TalkBack;
- **B, the home screen:** the home role, Home, a restart, the ways back from another app;
- **C, the lock:** screen pinning, and lock task as device owner;
- **D, the phone role:** the dialer role, an in-call service with a screen of its own, the call log.

**A scratch emulator of its own**, `ucap_m4_scratch`: arm64, Android 16 (API 36), the same Google Play image as the
shared emulator, made for M4 (no Google account on it, so a device owner is possible) and kept until M4 ends. The shared
emulator (`medium_phone`, whose test person holds the owner's real accounts) was never touched: every command named
the scratch emulator's serial (`tools/android/spikes/m4/drive.ts` finds it by its AVD's name).

**The spike app** (`tools/android/spikes/m4`, package `de.schaefchens.ucap.spike4`, built with the SDK's tools like
M3's): a launcher, home and dial activity, a device administrator, an in-call service with its own call screen, a
microphone service like LIVE's, and commands over `am start` and `am broadcast`. It logs names, states, counts and
times only. Afterwards: lock task ended, the device owner removed (`dpm list-owners`: none), the PIN cleared, the home
and phone roles back with Pixel Launcher and Google's Phone app, app pinning switched off again, the app uninstalled.

## A: Android's own unlock

| What | Measured |
|---|---|
| A key bound to the phone's lock, on a phone **without** a screen lock | refused: "Secure lock screen must be enabled to create keys requiring user authentication" |
| With a PIN (`locksettings set-pin`) | made in 39 ms (`setUserAuthenticationParameters(30 s, strong biometric or device credential)`); on the emulator in software (security level 0), on phones in the TEE or StrongBox |
| Signing without unlocking | refused once its window has passed: `UserNotAuthenticatedException` |
| Android's prompt (`BiometricPrompt`, biometric or device credential) | a sheet over the app with a PIN field typed on the **soft keyboard** (Gboard's number pad: three keys a row, about 2.5 cm by 1.5 cm); after the PIN the key signs for its window |
| The prompt **with TalkBack**, real finger events | **possible:** each digit found by touch and typed when the finger lifts (TalkBack types on lift on keyboards), then "Next" found and double-tapped: about 14 s for four digits. A first try that double-tapped every digit typed each twice and failed. Finding twelve small keys at the screen's bottom is the hard part |
| The prompt's secure window | shown black in screenshots; it is dismissed when TalkBack is suspended (uiautomator's dump), which a person never does |
| The phone's lock **removed** | Android **deletes the bound key**: it no longer exists |
| Fingerprint and face | not on this emulator (no enrolled finger): a real phone |

**Choice:** as the owner decided, UCAP's own code on its large areas is the main unlock. Android's lock is offered as
an extra only where the phone has a screen lock; its key can vanish whenever the person removes that lock, and then the
device falls back to UCAP's code and tells the server. A TalkBack user can unlock through Android's prompt, but only
by finding small keys on a keyboard: UCAP's four areas are far easier.

## B: UCAP as the home screen

| What | Measured |
|---|---|
| Android's question | "Set UCAP Spike 4 as your default home app?", the current one ("Pixel Launcher, Current default") and the app, "Cancel" / "Set as default": two choices, as M3's SMS role (K4) |
| Taking the role | Android starts the activity anew in a task of the home type: in the product the WebView would load again |
| Home | leads back to the app, from anywhere |
| After a restart | Pixel Launcher shows first, then the home app, **about 7 s after the boot completed**; Android still names it as the holder |
| Other apps the app can see | 21 with a `<queries>` entry for launchers (MAIN/LAUNCHER), no `QUERY_ALL_PACKAGES` |
| A notification "Zurück zu UCAP" while another app is in front | its tap brings the app back (the PendingIntent's creator allows the start, Android 15+) |
| **Coming to the front by itself** (a delayed start, after the shell's broadcast stopped lending its rights) | **allowed as the home app** (`BAL_ALLOW_ALLOWLISTED_COMPONENT`), **blocked otherwise** (`Background activity launch blocked`, `BAL_BLOCK`) |

**Choice:** "Lisa, zurück" by voice (G8) brings UCAP back only when it is the home screen; otherwise UCAP says that
Home or its notification do it. The home role is taken on the person's device after UCAP explains Android's question,
and the app must keep its state across the restart Android gives it when it becomes the home screen.

## C: the lock

**Screen pinning** (no device owner):

| What | Measured |
|---|---|
| Asking | `startLockTask()` opens a sheet: "App is pinned … Swipe up & hold to unpin. Personal data may be accessible … Pinned app may open other apps." with "No thanks" / "Got it" (a window of its own: only `uiautomator dump --windows` sees it) |
| The home app pinning itself | possible: UCAP can be the home screen and pinned |
| Pinned | Home, Back and the notification shade stay in the app; **another app is refused** (despite the sheet's wording) |
| Ending it | **swipe up and hold**: at once, without a PIN when the phone has none |

**Lock task as device owner** (`dpm set-device-owner …/.Admin`, which Android allowed because the emulator has no
account):

| What | Measured |
|---|---|
| Starting it | no question; `LOCKED` |
| An allowed app (the clock) | opens inside it; Home leads back to the app; Back from it too |
| An app not allowed (Settings) | refused: "Attempted Lock Task Mode violation" |
| Recents, the notification shade, swipe up and hold | do nothing |
| **Android's lock screen** with a PIN set | **does not come** inside lock task (the keyguard feature not granted); outside it, it does |
| `setKeyguardDisabled(true)` with a PIN set | refused (false): it only removes a lock screen without a PIN |
| **An incoming call** with Google's Phone app as the dialer | it rings, **but its screen never shows**: the person cannot answer inside the kiosk |
| Ending it and removing the owner | `stopLockTask()`, then `clearDeviceOwnerApp()`: no owner left |

**Choice:** the dedicated device's kiosk is lock task as device owner, the allowed apps in its list, Home leading to
UCAP, notifications and Android's lock screen only where chosen (G9). On any other device the lock is screen pinning,
and the page says plainly that one gesture ends it. A kiosk on a phone needs UCAP as the phone app (D).

## D: UCAP as the phone app

| What | Measured |
|---|---|
| Android's question | "Set UCAP Spike 4 as your default phone app?", two choices as above |
| What the role grants | `CALL_PHONE`, `READ_CALL_LOG`, `WRITE_CALL_LOG`, `READ_PHONE_STATE`, `READ_CONTACTS`, `ANSWER_PHONE_CALLS` |
| An incoming call (`adb emu gsm call`) | the in-call service is told 60 ms after it rings, and the app's own call screen is in front 110 ms later |
| Answer, speaker, hang up | `Call.answer`, `setAudioRoute(SPEAKER)`, `Call.disconnect`: active, then ended (cause LOCAL) |
| A missed call | ended with cause MISSED; in the call log as missed |
| Calling out (`TelecomManager.placeCall`) | no dialler in between: connecting, dialing, active, ended |
| A call **over Android's lock screen** | the app's own call screen shows over it (`showWhenLocked`); declined: cause REJECTED |
| A call **inside the kiosk** (lock task) | the app's own call screen shows inside it, answered and ended |
| The call log | each call with its kind (in, out, missed, declined) and length |

**Choice:** G10 builds the in-call service and a call screen in large areas; the call log is read as the role allows.
What waits for a real phone: a SIM's calls on a real network, Bluetooth headsets, emergency calls.
