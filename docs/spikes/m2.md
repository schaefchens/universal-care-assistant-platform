# M2 spikes: LIVE mode's listening

The measurements of `docs/plan-m2.md`, step L0: the voice detector, the keyword spotter, the microphone service. On
the Android emulator (arm64, Android 16, 4 cores) on the reference Mac (M1 Max), 2026-10-15. Vosk 0.3.75 (Android)
with its small German model 0.15 (Apache 2.0, 45 MB packed, 91 MB unpacked).

Run with `node tools/live/corpus.ts` (the recordings, into `~/.cache/ucap-bench`, never the repository) and
`node tools/live/bench.ts --model --push` (a debug build on the emulator).

## The recordings

Spoken by the Mac's German voices (`say`, 16 kHz mono), so no person's voice is in them:

| Set | What | Size |
|---|---|---|
| keyword | each candidate name by 9 voices, 5 ways each: alone ("Lisa."), "Hallo Lisa.", at the start of a question, of a request, inside a sentence ("Sag mal, Lisa, …") | 45 per name |
| nearmiss | 26 sentences with words close to the names ("Lies mir das vor", "Pause", "Hannes kommt heute") by 3 voices | 78 |
| speech | UCAP's own German texts by 2 voices | 70 min |
| other | German on other subjects (weather, sport, cooking, news, a quiz show, talk at the table: `tools/live/other-speech.ts`) by 4 voices | 67 min |
| noise | synthetic music (chords and a beat), pink noise, a quiet room | 12 min |

The ordinary words beside the keyword in its grammar are the most frequent of UCAP's own texts, so "speech" flatters
the spotter; "other" is the fair test of false wakes.

## B: voice activity — the app's energy rule; Silero is not needed

| Set | Silero VAD 6.2.3 (share of time called speech) | The app's energy rule |
|---|---|---|
| keyword recordings | 0.74 | 0.60 |
| near misses | 0.79 | 0.63 |
| UCAP's texts | 0.81 | 0.65 |
| synthetic music | 0.00 | 0.00 |
| pink noise | 0.00 | 0.02 |
| quiet room | 0.00 | 0.00 |

Both call noise and the synthetic music silence and every speech speech; neither tells the person from a television
(both are speech). What the detector is used for (cutting the open gate's utterances) needs no more than the energy
rule. Silero would add ONNX Runtime: 33 MB of native code per ABI. **Chosen: the energy rule**; ONNX Runtime is gone and
the app is 17 MB instead of 50. Silero's real-time factor was 0.005–0.02.

## C: the keyword spotter — Vosk with the keyword and 1000 ordinary words, at 0.6

Load: 0.4–0.6 s warm (4.3 s cold), 169 MB native memory. Real-time factor per keyword grammar: 0.015–0.04.

Four ways, five names, over the keyword recordings (heard: in how many of 45) and 70 minutes of UCAP's texts (false
wakes per hour), at confidence ≥ 0.5:

| Way | Lisa | Paul | Emma | Konrad | Hanna |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| the keyword alone in the grammar | 45, 914/h | 45, 507/h | 45, 778/h | 42, 409/h | 45, 760/h |
| the whole vocabulary (no grammar) | 5, 0/h | 19, 0/h | 11, 0/h | 3, 0/h | 0, 0/h |
| two stages (alone, confirmed by the vocabulary) | 5 | 19 | 11 | 3 | 0 |
| the keyword and 400 ordinary words | 22, 4.3/h | 35, 1.7/h | 26, 5.1/h | 35, 0/h | 39, 0/h |

The keyword alone: the decoder forces every word it hears onto it. The model's vocabulary hardly knows the names, so no
second stage can confirm them. The keyword with ordinary words beside it is the usable way.

|---|---|---|---|---|
| 45 of 45; 0 / 0 | 45; 1.8 / 0.9 | 44; 2.7 / 2.7 | 45; 6.3 / 4.5 | 36; 0 / 0 |

**Chosen:** 1000 ordinary words, confidence ≥ 0.6. Names of three syllables are heard far more reliably than short
ones and wake less: the suggestions in onboarding are **Mathilda, Theodor, Hanna, Leopold** (none a helper's name),
and the settings say that a name of three syllables is heard most reliably. Paul and Emma wake ten or more times an
hour on everyday speech and are not suggested.

The spotter hears every sample while the gate is closed, not only what a voice detector passes, so a quiet voice over
a television is not lost. The whole app then takes 0.6 % of one core on silence (0.18 s of CPU in 30 s) and 7 % while
the spotter hears radio speech (4.2 s in 60 s), on the emulator.

## A: the microphone service, and LIVE end to end on the emulator

The emulator's microphone is the Mac's: switched off (`adb emu avd hostmicoff`) so the capture never heard the room;
recordings were played into the running capture instead (a debug build's `VoiceCapture.inject`,
`tools/live/drive.ts`). Measured with the dev stack (whisper on the Mac, the dialogue model qwen3.6 on Ollama):

- The service runs in the foreground with type microphone and its notification "UCAP hört zu"; the keyword model came
  from the UCAP server (its digest checked) and was ready in about 2 s.
- "Mathilda, wie spät ist es?": the gate opened at the spotter's endpoint, the name was taken off, "Es ist 14:29 Uhr."
  about a second after the recording ended.
- "Und welcher Tag ist heute?" without the name: judged meant, answered.
- Two minutes of radio speech into the open gate: in the first runs the model took three sports sentences as meant and
  made notes of them. Since then what was heard without the name is judged in a short call of its own, an action after
  it asks first, and the utterance after the name said alone is judged too: 16 of 16 radio sentences dropped without a
  word.
- After three minutes without an exchange the gate closed by itself. Two minutes of radio with the gate closed: no
  turn, nothing sent, no false wake.
- "Mathilda, Hilfe!": asked back (A17); silence through both asks: A18b, nothing sent.
- "Pause": paused, radio meanwhile said nothing; "Mathilda, weiter." resumed.
- Speech over a long answer stopped it (barge-in), and the new question was answered.
- A reminder at 14:58: "Es ist 14:58 Uhr. Erinnerung: Trinken. Hast du etwas getrunken?"; "Ja." without the name was
  recorded as the person's answer.
- A new Telegram message was announced by LIVE word for word with its sender.

## What needs a real phone

- **How much of UCAP's own voice reaches the capture** with the platform's echo canceller (VOICE_COMMUNICATION and
  AcousticEchoCanceler are on): the emulator cannot tell; barge-in on a phone may also be triggered by UCAP's own
  voice where the canceller is weak.
- **Real voices**: the recordings are synthetic; older speakers and dialects may be heard less well.
- **Battery** with the microphone open all day (a bedside tablet is usually charging).
- **Android's background rules** on phone makers' systems (a microphone service started from the background is
  refused; LIVE then starts when the app is in front, and says so).
